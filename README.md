# TigerTag for OrcaSlicer

An OrcaSlicer 2.5 plugin that brings the spools of your
[TigerTag](https://github.com/TigerTag-Project) account into the slicer.

**Status: not started.** Planned:

1. Filament presets made from the spools in your account - material, colour,
   brand and temperatures.
2. A TigerTag tab in OrcaSlicer listing your spools and what is left on them,
   with a warning when a job needs more than the spool holds.

Requires OrcaSlicer 2.5 or later, whose plugin system is still new.
